# Scientific Discovery

A sample document for Foliograph smoke testing.

## Introduction

Scientific discovery is the process by which science and scientific knowledge
have been and continue to be developed. Each discovery represents a new
understanding of the natural world.

## The Scientific Method

The Scientific Method is a systematic approach to inquiry that forms the
foundation of modern science.

### Observation

Observation is the first step. Scientists record data from the natural world
using their senses and instruments.

### Hypothesis

A hypothesis is a testable prediction about the relationship between variables.
It forms the basis for experimental design.

### Experimentation

Experiments test hypotheses under controlled conditions. The Control Group
and Experimental Group are compared to isolate variables.

## Key Concepts

The Peer Review Process ensures scientific findings are validated by other
experts before publication. The Replication Crisis has highlighted the
importance of reproducible research.

## Conclusion

Scientific discovery depends on rigorous methodology, honest reporting,
and community validation. The Knowledge Graph of science grows with
each confirmed finding.
